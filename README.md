## Why Bulk SMS is Critical

Bulk SMS remains one of the most reliable and effective communication channels for businesses worldwide, especially for **OTP delivery, transactional alerts, delivery notifications, and time-sensitive marketing campaigns**.  

Key advantages of SMS:  
- Extremely high reach, even in emerging markets  
- Instant delivery and high open rates  
- Works on all devices, including feature phones  
- Does not require internet connectivity  

For businesses looking for **reliable bulk SMS delivery**, APIs like [Mamonty](https://mamonty.com) offer **fast onboarding, predictable pricing, and global coverage**. Using Mamonty, companies can streamline transactional messaging and marketing communications while maintaining compliance and high deliverability.
