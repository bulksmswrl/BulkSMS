## Compliance & Anti‑Spam Rules for Betting SMS

2026 brings stricter enforcement of **gambling SMS regulations**. Follow these rules to avoid blacklisting:

- **Explicit opt‑in** – never buy or scrape phone numbers.
- **Clear opt‑out instructions** – include `STOP` or `END` in every message.
- **Sender ID consistency** – do not spoof brands without permission.
- **Time‑of‑day restrictions** – avoid sending between 9 PM and 9 AM in most regions.
- **Affiliate liability** – if you send for white labels, ensure they provide consent proof.

Violations can lead to **permanent blocking** of your sending number or even fines up to $10,000 per message in some jurisdictions.
